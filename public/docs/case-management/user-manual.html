Daily procedures for household casework: correcting payment details, deactivating and reactivating households and members, tracking follow-ups and deciding pending updates. Case Management keeps why a household's record changed and who approved it. The household, its members and its payment account stay in the Registry and Payment modules; every change is made through the same rules from the web and the mobile app.

1. Purpose, Roles, and Access

A household changes over time: its mobile-money number goes wrong, a member dies, the family moves, a payment fails verification. Case Management lets staff record those changes with a reason, sends the ones that move money or end a membership to a second person, and keeps the full history.

Typical user roles

RoleTypical work
Field / district officer (PAA Facilitator, TMO, Council Coordinator)Propose payment corrections and deactivations; add follow-ups. Sees the households of their own districts.
Grievance officerWork the Account Corrections list.
Approver (e.g. Payment Approver, HQ)Approve or reject pending updates.
Supervisor / M&EDashboard and history.

Two-person rule. A change that moves money (account number, provider, account name) and every deactivation waits for a second person. Nobody can approve their own submission.

3. Dashboard

Four cards: Account Corrections (accounts that could not be verified), Open Follow-ups, Overdue (past their due date) and Pending Updates (awaiting a decision). Below them, follow-ups and pending updates by status, pending updates by source (web or mobile app), and case activity: payment changes, households deactivated, members deactivated. Click a card to open its list; Refresh reloads the figures.

4. Change Payment Details or Contact Phone

A household has two phone numbers, and only one of them pays:

FieldUsed forChanging it
Mobile money number / bank account numberWhere the money is sentNeeds a reason and approval; the account is verified again before the next payment.
Contact phoneReaching the household onlyApplies at once; never re-verifies the account. Recorded for review.

Correct payment details

  1. Open the household, go to the Case Management tab and choose Change payment details (or Edit payment details on a correction page).
  2. Change the account number, channel (Bank / Mobile money), provider or account name.
  3. Choose a Reason (e.g. Account number wrong or invalid) and add details.
  4. Press Save. The change is submitted for approval; the account keeps its current details until it is approved.
  5. After approval the account goes back to Pending verification and pre-audit, and is checked with GePG again.

Update the contact phone

Choose Update contact phone. Enter a Tanzanian mobile number (0712345678 or 255712345678); the dialog shows how it will be saved. Numbers must be 255 followed by 9 digits starting with 6 or 7.

Paid and in-progress accounts. Once an account has been paid it shows Locked · paid on {date}: its details change only through this dialog, with a reason and approval. While a payment to it is still on an unfinished paylist it shows Payment in progress and cannot be changed until the paylist finishes.

5. Account Corrections

Every account that failed verification, or was not sent because its number is invalid or belongs to another provider, is listed here until it is corrected. There is nothing to add or remove by hand: an account leaves the list once its approved correction sends it back for verification.

Number and provider don't matche.g. an Airtel number recorded as M-Pesa.
No accountShows the households that have no payment account at all; double-click opens the household.

Filter by Household ID, representative, FSP, channel or account / phone. A row marked Waiting for approval already has a correction submitted.

  1. Double-click a row to open the Account Correction page: account details, verification history (each attempt with its result and reason) and any change waiting for approval.
  2. Use Edit payment details or Update phone.
  3. While a correction waits, the page shows Now and After approval side by side, and a link to Pending updates.

6. Deactivate and Reactivate

Deactivation records a programme decision: the household or member stops being paid, but stays visible with its reason and history, and can be reactivated. It is not deletion, and it is not graduation.

Deactivate a household

  1. From the household's row action or its Case Management tab, choose Deactivate household.
  2. Choose the type, Temporary or Permanent, a reason and the Effective from date; add details.
  3. Press Deactivate and confirm. The request waits for approval; nothing changes until it is approved.
  • A Permanent deactivation is refused while members are still active, or while a payment to the household is in progress. The dialog lists the members to deactivate first.
  • Deceased applies to a household only when it has a single member.

Deactivate a member

  1. Choose Deactivate member on the member's row.
  2. Choose the reason (Shifted or Deceased) and the effective date; for Deceased, the date of death.
  3. If the member is the household's representative (payment recipient), choose the Successor who takes over.
  4. Confirm. The request waits for approval.
Recording a death ends the person's membership in every household they belong to and suspends their benefits. If it was recorded in error, reactivating the member reverses the whole change as one action.

Reactivate

Choose Reactivate household or Reactivate member. The menu shows why it was deactivated. The household returns to its previous status and can be paid again. Each deactivation and reactivation stays in the Deactivation history.

7. Follow-ups

A follow-up is a tracked piece of casework on a household, such as a payment failure to chase or a beneficiary to contact. Each has a category, priority, status, an assignee and a due date (7 days by default).

  1. Open Case Management → Follow-ups; filter by household, status, category or priority, or tick Overdue only.
  2. Open a follow-up to read it, then Update its status: Open → In progress → Resolved (or Escalated / Cancelled).
  3. When resolving, write a Resolution note. Open household goes to the household page.

8. Pending Updates (Approvals)

Every change that needs a decision is listed here, rated by severity: Critical (it moves money), Warning (e.g. a new representative) or Info (e.g. a contact phone). Changes sent from the mobile app are all listed here and change nothing until they are approved.

KindButtonsEffect
Waiting for approvalApprove / RejectApprove applies the change; Reject discards it.
Already appliedAcknowledge / FlagFlag opens a data-quality follow-up.
  1. Filter by kind, status, severity or source, and press Review on a row.
  2. Compare Before and After for each field.
  3. Write a decision note (required to reject or flag) and decide.

A district officer sees and decides only the households of their own districts. If an approved change could not be saved to the record, the row reads Approved but NOT applied and a data-quality follow-up is opened.

10. Troubleshooting and Good Practice

Message or problemWhat to do
“You submitted this change, so someone else must decide it.”By design; another approver must decide it.
“This change was already decided in the Tasks inbox.”Refresh the list.
Save is disabled: Payment in progressWait until the paylist holding the payment finishes.
Permanent deactivation refusedDeactivate the listed members first.
Someone else changed the record firstReopen the record and make the change again; edits are never merged silently.
A corrected account is still on Account CorrectionsThe correction is waiting for approval; see Pending updates.
Good practice. Put the contact number in Contact phone, never in the payment number. Always explain the reason in a sentence an auditor would understand. Work the Account Corrections list before each payment cycle.
