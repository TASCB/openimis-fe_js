How a payroll is created for a payment cycle, calculated, approved and handed to TASAF Payments. A payroll takes the enrolled households of a benefit plan and location, and the PCT rule works out each household's benefit. Once approved, it is paid through TASAF Payments.

3. Create a Payroll

  1. Open Payrolls and press +.
  2. Enter the Name and choose the Payment Plan (it sets the benefit plan and the PCT calculation), the Payment Point, the Payment Cycle (e.g. Sep – Oct 2026) and the Payment Method.
  3. Set Valid From and Valid To.
  4. Under Filter Criteria, choose the locations. Only beneficiaries of the selected plan and locations are included. Advanced Criteria narrow it further; a beneficiary must meet all of them.
  5. Press Save. The benefits are calculated in the background, and the payroll becomes Pending Approval with an approval task.
The household details are saved at calculation. Pre-audit later checks each amount against those saved details, so a change to a household after the payroll was calculated does not change its amount.

4. Review the Benefits

Open the payroll. The Benefit Consumptions tab lists one line per beneficiary with its code, individual, amount, date due and status; filter it by name, status or amount. The Tasks tab shows the payroll's approval task, and Payment Files any files attached.

5. Approve or Reject

  1. Open Tasks → Approve Payroll Tasks (or the payroll's Tasks tab).
  2. Open the task, check the number of beneficiaries and the total.
  3. Approve, or reject with a reason. A rejected payroll appears under Rejected Payrolls.

The person who created the payroll cannot approve it.

6. After Approval

An approved payroll is Approve for payment and appears under Approved Payrolls. It is paid through TASAF Payments:

  1. Pre-Audit checks the payment window's accounts, amounts and duplicates.
  2. Disbursement generates the Bank and MNO paylists from the payroll.
  3. The paylists are approved twice and submitted to MUSE; MUSE's replies mark each benefit paid or unapplied.

Do not use Make Payment or upload reconciliation files for TASAF payrolls: payment and reconciliation come from MUSE.

8. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
The payroll has fewer beneficiaries than expectedCheck the locations and criteria, and that the households are enrolled (active) in the benefit plan.
The payroll is not in TASAF Payments → DisbursementIt must be approved for payment first.
Benefits marked DuplicateAnother payroll of the same cycle already includes those households.
