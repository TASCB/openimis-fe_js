Payment Module

TASAF MIS — Operational End-User Manual

Tanzania Social Action Fund Mfuko wa Maendeleo ya Jamii Version 1.0 · 22 October 2026
Audience: Payment officers · pre-audit officers · paylist approvers · finance · auditors Module: openimis-be-tasaf_payment_py

Daily procedures for getting PCT benefits from an approved payroll to the households' bank and mobile-money accounts. The module checks every payment account with GePG, audits the payments of a payment window, builds the Bank and MNO batches (paylists), takes them through two approvals and sends them to MUSE, and follows each payment until it is paid or returned. Server set-up (GovESB keys, scheduled jobs) is covered in the administrator guide.

1. Purpose, Roles, and Access

The Payment module starts where Payroll ends: a payroll calculated by the PCT rule and approved for payment. It ends when MUSE reports every payment as paid or unapplied.

2. Navigation and the Payment Pipeline

Open Payments → TASAF Payments in the left menu. Everything in this manual is a tab of that one page:

TabUsed for
DashboardCounts of accounts and paylists at each stage.
VerificationChecking payment accounts with GePG.
Pre-AuditChecking a payment window before money moves.
DisbursementGenerating the Bank and MNO batches from a payroll.
PaylistsApproving and submitting batches; following MUSE's replies.
Unapplied PaymentsPayments the bank or operator could not credit.
ChargesBanks and mobile operators (FSPs), their names and withdrawal charges.
ReportsSummary of e-payment by FSP, with drill-down.
IntegrationsMUSE and GePG settings, and the changes awaiting approval.

The pipeline

  1. Verify accounts. Each household's primary account is checked with GePG, which returns the name the bank or operator holds. The system compares it with the household's recipient.
  2. Review name checks that are not a clear match.
  3. Pre-audit the payment window: account checks, amounts per household, duplicates.
  4. Generate the Bank and MNO paylists from the approved payroll.
  5. Approve each paylist twice, by two different people.
  6. Submit to MUSE. MUSE's replies update the paylist and each payment automatically.
  7. Follow up unapplied payments through Case Management.
Two-person rule. Paylists, and every change to MUSE or GePG settings, FSP details, FSP names and withdrawal charges, need a second person. The person who proposed a change can never approve it.

3. Verify Payment Accounts (GePG)

A payment account can only be paid once it is Verified. The Verification tab has five lists:

ListShows
Awaiting verificationAccounts not yet checked, or sent and waiting for GePG (Pending GePG).
SuccessVerified accounts. Accounts due their check before the first payment are marked Re-verify due.
FailedAccounts GePG or a reviewer rejected. They are fixed in Case Management → Account Corrections.
Needs reviewAccounts whose returned name does not clearly match (section 4).
GePG requestsEvery request sent to GePG, with its answer.

Send accounts

  1. Open Awaiting verification and filter by location, FSP or account type.
  2. Tick accounts and press Verify, or press Verify all matching (N) to send every account that matches the filters, on every page.
  3. Confirm. A summary shows what was sent, refused, skipped, or withdrawn because the number is invalid or belongs to another operator.
  4. Results arrive when GePG answers. You get a notification for each request; the accounts move to Success, Failed or Needs review.
Wrong-operator numbers are not sent. A mobile number whose prefix belongs to another operator (e.g. an Airtel number recorded as M-Pesa), or that is not 255 + 9 digits, is withdrawn, marked Failed and listed in Case Management → Account Corrections.

Show payload

Show payload (⋮ menu) shows exactly what Verify all matching would send for the current filters. Nothing is sent and no account changes.

Re-verify before the first payment

Every account is checked once more just before it is first paid. On Success, use Re-verify before payment (ticked rows) or Re-verify all due. Until GePG answers, the account cannot pass pre-audit.

GePG requests

Each row is one request to one FSP, with its accounts and results (verified, to review, failed). An account in a request is Pending GePG and cannot be sent again. If GePG never answers, press Release: the accounts go back to Awaiting verification. A late answer is still applied.

4. Review Name Checks

When the name returned by the bank or operator does not clearly match the household's recipient, the account goes to Needs review. Open it to see the recipient name in the MIS, the name returned, the match score and the reason (e.g. Surname differs, Name of another household member), plus earlier attempts.

  1. Compare the names with supporting documents if needed.
  2. Press Confirm match: the account becomes Verified and goes on to pre-audit and payment.
  3. Or press Reject: it becomes Failed and goes to Case Management → Account Corrections.
  4. Add a note on why; it is kept in the audit trail.

5. Pre-audit a Payment Window

At the top of Pre-Audit, choose the payment window (the payment cycle, e.g. Sep – Oct 2026). Every tab looks at all payrolls of that window except rejected ones.

5.1 Overview

Cards for Total MNOs, Total Banks and Total, each with the amount, households and payments. A note below counts payments whose household has no primary payment account. To look at one household, type its HHID (or part of it) and press Open household.

5.2 Accounts: run the account checks

Only accounts that Passed are paid. Tick accounts (or all) and press Run Pre-Audit.

5.3 Compliance: is each household paid the right amount?

Every payment is recalculated from the household details saved when the payroll was calculated:

PartRule
Base14,000
Disability5,000 once per household
Health7,000 per child aged 0–5, at most 3
Primary5,000 per primary student, at most 3
Secondary8,000 per secondary student, at most 3
Household cap50,000 in total, then penalties are deducted

Four cards count Match, Overpaid, Underpaid and Data error (a payment that cannot be checked). A table shows each part for the whole window; below it, the payments that do not match, with the expected and paid amounts and the part that differs. Recalculate refreshes the figures; Export downloads every payment part by part (CSV).

Click a match to see its payments, and an HHID to open the household.

5.5 The household view

Shows the head, village, payment account and its status; the Findings in plain sentences; one table per payment with Recorded (saved at calculation), Expected (the rule applied to it) and Today (the rule applied to the members now, changed parts in red); the members; and every payment of the household in all windows.

Findings do not stop a paylist yet. They are for review. Clearing or excluding a finding with a second person's approval is planned.

6. Generate a Disbursement Batch

A paylist is one batch sent to MUSE, either Bank or MNO, never both; one payroll normally gives two. Very large batches are split into parts of at most 50,000 payments. A benefit is paid from one paylist only.

  1. Select Payroll: only payrolls approved for payment are listed.
  2. Batch Type & Scope: Bank or MNO, and the destination.
  3. Review: what the batch will hold. Nothing is created yet.
  4. Generate: creates the paylist(s) as Pending Approval. Nothing is sent.

Reading the Review step

SectionMeaning
Will be included (green)Payments and amount, by FSP.
Warnings (amber)The batch can be generated but will run into trouble: an FSP without MUSE details (Submit will be refused), or no withdrawal-charge band for an amount (the household would get less). Fix them on the Charges tab first.
Needs attentionHouseholds left out, and where to fix them: account not verified, pre-audit not passed, invalid mobile number, no primary account.
Not in this batch (grey)Paid through the other channel, or already on a paylist. No action needed.
Households left out are not lost. Once fixed, generate again for the same payroll: only households not yet on a paylist are picked up.

7. Approve and Submit Paylists

On Paylists, double-click a paylist to open it. The header shows the batch type, status, MUSE batch reference and MUSE's last reply; the summary shows the gross amount sent to MUSE, the net amount to households and the withdrawal charges.

  1. Approve Paylist, twice, by two different people. After the first approval it stays Pending Approval; after the second it is Approved. Approvals can also be given from the Tasks inbox.
  2. Press MUSE message to see the exact message and anything MUSE would reject in it. Nothing is sent. Fix every listed problem.
  3. Press Submit to MUSE and confirm. The paylist becomes Submitted to MUSE only once it has really been sent.
  4. MUSE's replies move it to Received, Accepted and Sent to bank / MNO, and mark each payment paid or unapplied. When every payment has an outcome the paylist is Closed.
Submit is refused, with the reason, when MUSE would reject the message, when some payments are already on another paylist being paid, or when GovESB is not set up on the server. If the connection fails, press Submit again: the same message is resent, so nobody is paid twice.

Rejected at approval is final: its households are released for a new batch. Rejected by MUSE can be fixed and submitted again. Export Paylist (PDF) and Export items (CSV) download the batch.

8. Unapplied Payments

A payment the bank or operator could not credit (wrong or closed account, name mismatch, returned by the bank) is listed here with MUSE's reason code and description. These records come from MUSE and cannot be edited.

  1. Open the payment and find its account.
  2. Correct the account in Case Management → Account Corrections; it is then verified again.
  3. The household is paid in a later batch once the account passes again.

9. Payment Dashboard

Verification Status counts accounts that are Pending, Awaiting GePG, Verified, in Manual Review and Failed. Paylist Status has five cards with count and amount: Pending Approval, Approved, With MUSE, Rejected by MUSE and Closed. Click a card to open the list behind it.

At the start of a cycle, bring Pending and Awaiting GePG close to zero and clear Manual Review before generating paylists.

10. Charges: FSPs and Withdrawal Charges

Each household is paid its benefit plus the withdrawal charge of its bank or operator, so it can withdraw the full entitlement. The Charges tab lists every bank and mobile operator (FSP).

StatusMeaning
ReadyNothing blocks payments to this FSP.
No charge bandsPayments to its accounts are refused at generation.
No band for 19,001–19,999A gap: a payment in that range is refused.
MUSE details missingA bank without bank name or BIC: Submit to MUSE is refused.
Awaiting MUSEA mobile operator without a BIC from MUSE yet.
Awaiting approvalA change to it waits for a second person.
No accountsNo payment account uses it.

Tick Only FSPs that need attention to hide the rest.

Edit an FSP

Click a row (or ✎) to open it:

  • Details: code, name, channel, MUSE bank name, BIC; for operators, the mobile prefixes (e.g. 068, 069, 078).
  • Other names: other spellings found on accounts (e.g. "M-Pesa", "Vodacom M-Pesa"), all treated as this FSP.
  • Charge bands: the charge for each amount range. A gap between bands is shown in red as you edit.
  1. Make the change, add a reason, and press Submit for approval.
  2. The FSP shows Awaiting approval and cannot be changed again until the change is decided.
  3. A second person approves it under Integrations → MUSE → Changes; only then does it apply.

Import charge bands (CSV)

The ⬆ icon imports a file with columns EPAYMENT_CODE, LOWER_AMOUNT, UPPER_AMOUNT, WITHDRAWAL (Download template gives an example). Choose Add to current bands or Replace the bands of these FSPs; FSPs not in the file are never touched. Each FSP in the file becomes one change awaiting approval. A file with any bad row, or with overlapping bands, is refused as a whole.

Adding an FSP (the round + button) sends two changes for approval: its name and its MUSE details. An FSP can be deleted only when no account uses it.

11. Reports

Summary of e-Payment by FSP: one row per FSP with households, withdrawal charges, the PCT benefit parts and the total paid. Only payments the gateway has confirmed are counted. Benefit parts are before the household cap, so they can add up to more than was paid.

  1. Select an FSP to see the payments behind its row; Include unpaid adds returned and pending ones.
  2. Select a beneficiary to see the benefit breakdown and every payment ever attempted.
  3. Download exports the summary as CSV with a totals row.

12. Integrations: MUSE and GePG

MUSE

The institution code, payer account, sub-budget classes, payment description, straight-through processing and GL accounts used in every message to MUSE. Below them, Changes lists every proposed change (MUSE settings, FSP details, other names, charges) with what changes from what to what. For charge bands, only the bands that differ are shown: removed ones struck through, then the new ones.

  1. The maker edits, gives a reason and presses Submit for approval.
  2. A checker presses Approve or Reject (with a comment). The maker can Cancel request.

GePG

TASAF's SP code, SP system ID and Service code at GePG, all supplied by GePG, changed with the same two-person rule. Warnings at the top appear only when something needs fixing (GovESB not configured, no API code yet, settings approved on another server, providers skipped because they have no BIC). Verification requests is the same list as Verification → GePG requests.

14. Troubleshooting and Good Practice

ProblemWhat to do
My payroll is not in Select PayrollOnly payrolls approved for payment are listed. Have it approved first.
A paylist has fewer households than expectedRead Needs attention on the Review step: the accounts are not verified, not pre-audited, have invalid numbers, or there is no primary account.
An account cannot be sent againIt is waiting for GePG. Release its request on GePG requests.
No answer from GePG after a dayRelease the request and send again; tell the administrator if it repeats.
Submit to MUSE is refusedOpen MUSE message and fix what it lists; check the FSP's status on Charges.
A change I made is not appliedIt waits for a second person under Integrations → MUSE → Changes.
I cannot approve my own changeBy design; another approver must decide it.
Good practice. Pre-audit every window before generating. Check the Charges tab for No band and MUSE details missing before the cycle. Always open MUSE message before submitting.
