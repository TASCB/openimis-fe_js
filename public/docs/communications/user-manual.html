3. Create an Activity

  1. Open Activities and press + (or New activity on the calendar).
  2. Under Planning — before the activity: code, title, activity type (e.g. Media Mission, Press Release, Community Awareness Session), programme area, description, objective, start and end, venue or virtual platform, location, target audience and planned audience.
  3. Watch the conflict banner: Conflicts that block saving (red) or Warnings.
  4. Press Save. The tabs appear; they can only be filled after the first save.
  5. After the activity, fill Outcome — recorded after the activity: actual audience and media houses reported. Some figures are worked out from the Audience tab or the participant register.

6. Events and the Participant Register

An event is created the same way under Events, with an event type: Public, Internal or Corporate Social Responsibility, and can be virtual or hybrid.

  1. Open the event's Participants tab and press Add participant: full name, stakeholder type, organisation and designation, phone, email. A participant can be linked to a beneficiary.
  2. Or choose Invite from a stakeholder list to add every member of a saved list at once.
  3. Set each person's attendance: Invited, Confirmed, Attended, Absent or Replaced.

The tab shows how many are on the register and how many attended; the actual audience of the event is taken from it.

7. News Feed and Announcements

Post an update

  1. Open News Feed and click Post an update….
  2. Optionally start from a template. Write the title and message; the editor has headings, bold, colour, links, images, lists and alignment. Attach files if needed.
  3. Choose the type: Announcement, Update, Media Highlight or Newsletter.
  4. Press Post (or Save draft). Posts wait for a communication officer's approval before others see them; the feed shows how many are awaiting approval.

Posts can be pinned to the top, edited, unpublished or deleted from the ⋮ menu. The search box looks through the latest posts.

Announcements at login

Published announcements a user has not read yet open in a window after they log in, one after the other (Announcement 1 of 3). Read moves to the next.

8. Media Houses and Journalists

Media houses

Each media house has a name, category, medium (newspaper, radio, TV, social media, website, blog), reach scope, language, frequency or channel, location, contact person, phone, email, website, and a primary journalist. Its page shows its coverage records and their quality.

Journalists

First and last name, gender, media house (or Freelance), role (Reporter, Editor, Presenter, Producer, Camera Operator, Photographer, Correspondent), beat, languages, phones, email, and accreditation number and expiry. Its page shows the stories filed and the last coverage.

9. Library

TabHolds
TemplatesA name, subject and message that fill a News Feed post when chosen. Inactive templates are hidden from the composer.
AssetsBrand assets, images, videos and documents for reuse.
Stakeholder listsNamed lists of people (name, organisation, location, phone, email) to invite to events. Deleting a list leaves people already invited on their events.

10. Calendar and Dashboard

The Calendar shows activities in month, week or agenda view, filtered by status, with Training and Coordination activities alongside so clashes are visible. The Dashboard summarises activities by status and type, audience reach and media coverage.

11. Troubleshooting and Good Practice

ProblemWhat to do
The tabs are missingSave the activity first.
Save is disabledRead the red conflict banner and change the time, venue or staff.
My post is not visible to othersIt is awaiting approval.
An attachment fails to uploadThe file is too large; reduce it or upload a link.
Good practice. Record media coverage with its link the same week. Keep stakeholder lists up to date so event invitations take one click.
